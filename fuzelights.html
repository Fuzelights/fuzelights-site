Fuzelights

Fuzelights are LED lights built inside antique Edison-base glass plug fuses: salvaged bits of electrical history, lit up again.

They sip power, costing pennies a year to run, and they last. One has been glowing nonstop for more than 12 years.

Available in a range of colors. Ask about a set for your own old fuse panel.
