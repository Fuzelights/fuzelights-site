SMART Panels

Every pattern in this panel is real. The stainless steel sheet is a solder paste stencil, laser-cut to manufacture circuit boards for aircraft avionics. Each tiny opening matches a pad where an electronic component was soldered.

Paste was squeegeed through the stencil onto a bare board, a machine placed hundreds of parts, and an oven melted the paste to lock everything in place. When those boards were retired, the stencils had nothing left to do.

Now they're backlit as art, a one-of-a-kind map of the electronics that once flew.
